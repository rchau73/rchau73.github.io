An engineering team made of AI agents

I run an engineering team made of AI agents on optionsBot, a trading system on an exchange's test environment. This film follows one real ticket through it, and shows what I still decide myself.

About this page. It shows the agentic approach: how AI agents and people share the work. It is not a detailed account of the implementation. For the scripts, the agents' rules and the full workflow, see the repository on GitHub.

What the film shows

  1. 0:04
    One ticket, five roles. Issue #553: a repair picked a strike from a broken quote. The domain-expert agent spots it in the live bots and files the issue with evidence. The product-manager agent places it in the strategy epic and moves it to Ready. The Dev agent writes the fix and its tests and opens PR #556. The Reviewer agent checks the diff against the repository's rules. It changes a trading rule, which is policy, so I read it and merge it.
  2. 0:16
    Agents propose everything. A person merges what carries risk. Docs and monitor pages, tests and tooling, and agent-workflow fixes ship on their own once reviewed clean with CI green. Trading and risk rules, configuration and CI, agent permissions and every release to stable wait for me.
  3. 0:24
    One month of delivery, October 2026. Over 300 pull requests merged, 167 of them written by the Dev agent. The film's other two figures come from the last week of October, on mvp, the test environment: 4 minutes from merge to live (median) and a 1 % change failure rate (1 of 85 changes).
  4. 0:32
    AI writes the code. I decide what ships.
