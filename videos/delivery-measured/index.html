Delivery, measured

How fast does a team of AI agents ship? I don't guess; I measure it, with the four DORA metrics per environment.

About this page. It shows the agentic approach: how AI agents and people share the work. It is not a detailed account of the implementation. For the scripts, the agents' rules and the full workflow, see the repository on GitHub.

What the film shows

  1. 0:04
    The four DORA metrics, week of 26 October 2026, mvp. 84 deploys in one week; 4 minutes from merge to live (median, p90 7 minutes); 1 % change failure rate (1 of 85); 57 minutes to restore (one incident). Elite on three of the four by DORA bands; time to restore has one sample, too few to rate.
  2. 0:13
    One change, 30 October 2026 (UTC). 18:55 issue #553 filed with evidence; 19:31 the Dev agent opens PR #556 with tests; 19:40 it changes a trading rule, so I merge it; 19:45 live on mvp, the test environment. From a filed issue to live in 50 minutes, with a person on the risky step.
  3. 0:24
    Two speeds, on purpose. mvp: 4 minutes from merge to live, on every merge. stable: 3.1 days median, because every release soaks 3 days on mvp first.
  4. 0:32
    Fast where it's safe. Slow where it matters.
