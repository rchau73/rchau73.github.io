The product the agents built

What the agents built: a monitor for an options bot running on Deribit testnet, designed to give the answer first and to show losses as clearly as gains.

About this page. It shows the product the agents built, at a glance. It is not a detailed account of the implementation. For the scripts, the agents' rules and the full workflow, see the repository on GitHub.

What the film shows

  1. 0:04
    Overview. The answer first, coin before dollars: what it earned, what is open, the worst case and the margin used.
  2. 0:10
    Each bot. Each bot's market and positions, and a one-line reason for its last trade.
  3. 0:16
    Positions: a short strangle. A call and a put, sold together: what it pays at expiry, what it costs to close now, and the bot's exit lines.
  4. 0:22
    One leg, on purpose. In a negative gamma regime with a bear trend, the bot stops selling puts, and the page says why.
  5. 0:28
    Worst case. Risk in plain words: what a very bad day would cost, in coin and dollars, and what the estimate can't promise.
  6. 0:34
    Income. Losses shown as clearly as gains: October is a loss so far on testnet, and the page says so in the same place as the gains.
  7. 0:40
    Activity. What the bot did, and why: every decision comes from the bot's journal, written in plain English.
  8. 0:46
    Assistant. An AI assistant answers from the docs and the bot's own journal, and links its sources.
  9. 0:52
    Built by agents. Made for people.
