What an AI reviewer catches

Every pull request on optionsBot gets a review from a Reviewer agent. Here is what 158 of those reviews caught, and what they can't do.

About this page. It shows the agentic approach: how AI agents and people share the work. It is not a detailed account of the implementation. For the scripts, the agents' rules and the full workflow, see the repository on GitHub.

What the film shows

  1. 0:04
    158 reviews, by verdict. 132 clean, 19 blocked a merge, 4 runs failed and were retried, 3 incomplete and re-run. About 1 in 8 reviews stopped a change before it merged.
  2. 0:13
    Three of the 19 blocks. PR #241: a deploy could lose the realised P&L of a close that filled while resting. PR #271: re-running setup regenerated the admin password, so sign-in failed. PR #551: the rebuild would have reverted two merged features.
  3. 0:24
    What it can't do. Decide whether a trading rule is right: those changes wait for me. Review files it didn't read: 3 reviews said so and were run again. Replace the pipeline: CI still gates every merge.
  4. 0:32
    A second pair of eyes. Not the last one.
